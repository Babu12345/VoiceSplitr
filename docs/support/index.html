Help for VoiceSplitr

Receipt Not Scanning Properly

If the app isn't reading your receipt correctly:

  • Good lighting: Make sure the receipt is well-lit and not blurry.
  • Flat surface: Place the receipt on a flat surface to avoid wrinkles and shadows.
  • Full receipt: Capture the entire receipt including all items, tax, and total.
  • Edit after scanning: You can manually edit any items, prices, or quantities that were parsed incorrectly on the Review screen.

Voice Input Not Working

If the app isn't recognizing your speech:

  • Check microphone permission: Go to Settings > VoiceSplitr and ensure microphone access is enabled.
  • Check speech recognition permission: The app also needs Speech Recognition permission to transcribe your voice.
  • Speak clearly: Mention item names as they appear on the receipt for best matching.
  • Edit transcripts: You can tap on any saved transcript to edit the speaker name or text before processing.

Items Assigned to Wrong Person

If the AI assigned items incorrectly:

  • Edit assignments: Tap "Edit" on the Split Results screen to reassign items using checkboxes.
  • Be specific: When recording, mention specific item names from the receipt (e.g., "I had the fish and chips bowl").
  • Name everyone: Say who got what clearly (e.g., "I got the soup, and Anna got the drinks").
  • Shared items: Say "we shared" or "we split" to assign an item to multiple people.

Prices Look Wrong

If the prices don't match your receipt:

  • Check the Review screen: You can edit all item prices, quantities, tax, and tip before proceeding.
  • Quantity items: Items with quantities (x2, x3) show the per-unit price. The total is calculated automatically.
  • Tax & tip: These are split proportionally based on each person's share of the subtotal.
